# Create a post

1. Open **Content** and find the blog.
2. Create a post and enter its name.
3. Fill in the title, introduction and article body.
4. Select a suitable image from Media.
5. Save and preview before publishing.

## A useful introduction

Tell the reader what the story covers in two short sentences. Avoid repeating the title.

| Field | Editorial guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Specific and easy to scan |
| Introduction | A short reason to keep reading |
| Main text | Headings, short paragraphs and useful links |
| Image | Relevant subject and meaningful alternative text |

## Check your work

- [ ] The title describes the story.
- [ ] All links work.
- [ ] Images have useful alternative text.

Continue to the [approval flow](../publishing/workflow.md#approval-flow).
