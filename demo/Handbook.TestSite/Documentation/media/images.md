# Choose an image

Upload images in **Media** and choose them from the image picker in your post.
Use descriptive names and avoid uploading several copies of the same photo.

![An example editorial illustration](../images/editorial-desk.png)

## Alternative text

Describe the information an image adds. For example, "A notebook and cup on the editorial desk" is more useful than "image123".
